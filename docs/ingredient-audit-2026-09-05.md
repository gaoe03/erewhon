# Ingredient audit, September 5, 2026

Reviewed all 124 profiles appearing in one or two smoothies before this pass, using all their archived source labels. This is an identity and classification audit, not a fresh recipe scrape or a nutritional equivalence assessment. Ambiguous product types were checked against the primary sources below.

The resulting directory has 208 used profiles, down from 216. There are 117 profiles used by one or two smoothies after consolidation and corrections. Those remaining entries are not errors merely because they are uncommon. The categories remain the existing 11.

Collagen, marine collagen, collagen with pearl, greens and collagen, and collagen blends now share Collagen. This also consolidates the related marine and pearl profiles above the low-use threshold for consistency. All listed products remain in the recipe, including when two share a display row. The 33-smoothie count counts each drink once. Copina is a plant-based creamer with no actual collagen peptides and remains separate.

22 distinct raw labels changed profile. No source recipes changed. Old IDs redirect directly to the current profiles. The audit also corrects Harmless Harvest cup yogurt to Coconut yogurt, distinguishes flaxseed from flax oil, and removes the unsupported edible-gold description.

## Evidence and boundaries

- [Copina wholesale FAQ](https://copinacowholesale.com/products/bulk-90-serving-vanilla-plant-based-collagen-boost-creamer-drink-blend) explicitly states that its plant-based blends contain no collagen peptides and identifies the vanilla product as a creamer.
- [Thorne collagen peptides](https://www.thorne.com/ingredients/collagen-peptides) identifies bovine collagen and lists Collagen Plus. [Ancient Nutrition](https://ancientnutrition.com/pages/merit-rapid-hydration) describes actual collagen together with electrolytes. These support grouping under Collagen, not a claim of identical formulas.
- [JSHealth Greens + Collagen](https://uk.jshealthvitamins.com/products/greens-collagen-formula) identifies greens with marine collagen.
- [Beekeeper's Naturals Superfood Honey](https://www.beekeepersnaturals.com/products/b-powered-330g) identifies a honey base with other bee ingredients. Honey is the browsing group, not a claim of pure honey.
- [Codeage Hair Vitamins](https://www.codeage.com/collections/wellness/products/hair-vitamins-keratin-biotin-collagen) lists a multivitamin formula with additional supplements. The profile does not promise a hair-related effect.
- [The Fullest saffron latte recipe](https://thefullest.com/blogs/journal/saffron-latte-recipe) and [Blume's latte preparation](https://itsblume.com/pages/fall-bundle) establish latte powders rather than plain seasoning.
- [Harmless Harvest's 2022 product announcement](https://harmlessharvest.com/blogs/press-releases/harmless-harvest-debuts-organic-dairy-free-coconut-yogurt-alternative-portfolio) identifies its vanilla cup as coconut yogurt.
- The stored label Gold Dust (Keto Brown Sugar & Turmeric) directly contradicts the former edible-gold description. The exact reviewed label maps to Spiced sugar topping. A future bare Gold Dust label requires review.

Current manufacturer pages establish product type. They do not establish historical amounts or unchanged formulations. This audit does not import health claims from product marketing.

## Future refresh reviews

Run `node scripts/ingredient-audit.mjs --low-use` before accepting new ingredient definitions. Low counts should prompt a review of product type, source wording and nearby profiles. They should never trigger automatic merging. Reuse reviewed generic profiles, preserve full menu variants, keep direct aliases and test both the intended match and the closest incorrect merge.
